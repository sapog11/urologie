About the clinic

MenCare clinic is a private urology clinic in Prague specialising in men's health.

If you are looking for an experienced urologist in Prague — you are in the right place. The clinic sees men of all ages for any urological concern, from routine check-ups to complex surgical cases.

Appointments are conducted in a calm, confidential environment. No queues. By prior arrangement. The doctors consult in English, Russian and Czech.
