About the clinic

MenCare clinic is a private urology clinic in Prague specialising in men's health.

This is an authored project by Evžen Zorič, created in collaboration with the head of the urology department, MUDr. Jiří Kočárek Ph.D. — a partnership of over 10 years.

If you are looking for an experienced urologist in Prague — you are in the right place. The clinic sees men of all ages for any urological concern, from routine check-ups to complex surgical cases.

Appointments are conducted in a calm, confidential environment. No queues. By prior arrangement. The doctors consult in English, Russian and Czech.

How your visit works

  1. 1

    Consultation and review of your records

    The doctor listens to your concerns and goes through your medical records: reports from your GP, other urologists and any test results you have.

  2. 2

    Ultrasound of the urinary tract

    The ultrasound is done during the same visit — no separate appointment and no travelling elsewhere.

  3. 3

    Clinical examination

    A gentle examination in a calm setting, one to one with the doctor. No unpleasant or invasive procedures.

  4. 4

    Findings and treatment plan

    The doctor explains the results in plain language, prescribes treatment and gives recommendations. Blood and urine tests are added if needed.

How to prepare

  • Bring your medical records from your GP or other urologists, along with any test results you already have — a urine test at minimum.
  • Important: come with a full bladder — this is essential for a proper bladder ultrasound.

Care does not end when you leave: for any question before or after treatment you can reach the managing director of the clinic directly. The line is open around the clock — in an urgent situation you will not be left without an answer.

Main areas of care

  • Prevention, treatment and care for men's urological health
  • Care led by Dr. Kočárek — over 30 years of practice
  • Professional diagnostics: ultrasound, lab tests, full check-up
  • Surgical and conservative treatment of prostate conditions: prostatitis, prostate adenoma (BPH)
  • Kidney and bladder health, treatment of stones
  • Treatment of cystitis and urogenital infections
  • Preventive programmes and check-ups
  • Laboratory tests
